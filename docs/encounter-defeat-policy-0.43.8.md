# Narrative survival and encounter defeat — 0.43.8

Mist Escape and lich Rejuvenation describe what happens after defeat; they do not keep their side active in the current tactical encounter.

- A vampire at 0 HP counts as defeated for encounter victory even if Mist Escape is pending or its mist form remains on the scene.
- A lich at 0 HP counts as defeated even though Rejuvenation can restore it later.
- Mist movement, immunity and escape mechanics remain available while another active combatant keeps the encounter running. This preserves narrative handling without requiring the opposition to chase an untargetable 0-HP form.
- Living, hidden, invisible, distant and temporarily unreachable opponents still prevent victory as before.

This is a table-adjudication policy, not a claim that PF2e removes either creature's narrative survival ability. It prevents the observed state in which two victorious liches passed turns through round 120 because four 0-HP vampire mists never completed their escape.
