Native manual saves share these condition resolvers; damage remains with the normal manual damage workflow. AI casting also resolves Soothe and sustained Forbidding Ward. The narrower spell coverage described below records earlier releases.

Version **0.39.0** implements the [GM's condition policy](condition-precedence.md): Fleeing > Confused > Controlled, a shared escape driver for taught NPCs, manual Fleeing action legality, and newest-source replacement for fascination. Earlier limitations below about universal compelled-action handling remain historical; Controlled alone still needs its controller's direction.

Patch **0.37.1** serializes owned spell-effect mutations across turn/time hooks, application, Sustain, End and Reset. This prevents simultaneous expiry requests from deleting the same Laughing Fit source twice. The reaction barrier continues to await all jobs and permit nested reactions; it is not used as a mutex. Real permission or database failures still surface, and subsequent cleanup can retry.

Version 0.35.0 adds separate [AI condition rules](condition-ai.md) and [manual character automation](condition-manual.md). Version **0.37.0** adds the [Confused NPC attack strategy](confused-ai.md), with random targets across alliances and ordinary attacks/basic preparation only. Confused also denies supported manual reactions. The [rules/module audit](condition-rules-audit-2026-11-06.md) records ownership, current errata, native verification and remaining manual cases. Manual flat checks fall back to this module when PF2e Automations' flat-check setting is off, as it was in the audited arena world.

The world setting **Enforce action and reaction restrictions** defaults on. It connects prepared PF2e conditions and PF2e Automations 1.4.10 flags to the existing manual and AI workflows. The installed external markers use `flags.pf2e.noUseReactions` and `flags.pf2e.noActions`; effect names and imported prose are not parsed as rules.

## Restrictions

No Reactions prevents Creature AI reaction offers and reservations, including Reactive Strike, Shield Block, Goblin Scuttle, Sweeping Hook and Arrest a Fall. It also gates explicit manual reaction checks and actual-use reaction cards. When manual native damage requests an unavailable Shield Block, damage is applied without that block. Denial neither spends a reaction nor refreshes one already spent. Removing the restriction restores eligibility only if the ordinary reaction is otherwise available.

No Actions additionally prevents supported manual action-labelled checks, spellcasting through native spellcasting entries before slot consumption, and voluntary token movement. Saving throws, flat checks, initiative, unlabelled non-action checks and rerolls remain available. Forced displacement, falls, undo and teleport placement are not treated as voluntary actions. As GM, a custom movement operation can explicitly supply `foundry-creature-ai:ignore-action-restrictions` when resolving a forced placement.

An affected NPC completes its current turn and can auto-advance, while retaining its existing action budget if the restriction is removed before advancement. Temporary inability to act is separate from encounter defeat; a No Actions effect cannot falsely declare the battle won. Expired granted-marker parents do not leave the reaction restriction active in this resolver.

Blinded is an observer-relative perception restriction, not a ban on acting. Ordinary hearing treats non-Undetected opponents as Hidden, retaining a known square. The NPC can approach that square and target it with a DC 11 flat check. A successful Sneak or other Undetected result prevents tracking live movement; only remembered positions and the encounter's known roster inform planning. Hearing Seek rolls native secret Perception against Stealth DCs and can locate an Undetected creature as Hidden, even on a critical success. It cannot improve Hidden to Observed, so the NPC does not repeat Seek for an impossible improvement. Bounded Seek can search the observer's nearby area without aiming at unknown coordinates. Simplified field Seek retains the configured whole-field scope and uses hearing while blind; it can hear through ordinary sight-blocking walls, while physical barriers still block attacks.

Blinded plus Deafened disables ordinary hearing; prepared, in-range nonvisual senses can still locate creatures according to their acuity. Sight upgrades such as darkvision do not bypass blindness. Strikes against a defender unable to precisely perceive the attacker apply off-guard contextually, without creating a global Off-Guard condition. A heard Hidden creature can trigger Reactive Strike, which awaits the DC 11 check and spends the reaction on failure. Undetected creatures do not trigger these automated reactions. Manual attacks use the same targeting/defense pipeline. Normal terrain becomes difficult in both native movement measurement and AI planning; ordinary difficult terrain does not stack, greater terrain remains greater, and forced displacement/teleportation are preserved. Step availability follows the native measured cost.

Dazzled attackers use DC 5. Hidden and scene concealment checks take the stronger applicable DC. PF2e handles numeric condition penalties and visual immunity; native validation confirmed Blinded's −4 Perception penalty. Unusual sense exceptions, silence effects and adjudicated sensory environments may still require GM correction. Enabled PF2e Automations flat checks own manual spell cards; when that setting is off, Creature AI supplies the manual fallback. Managed casting retains its own awaited checks.

Cover/concealment/Hidden captions remain relative to the single selected observer (or assigned character when nothing is selected). More than one selected token is ambiguous and clears these captions. Blinded observers show `Hidden (observer blinded)` where appropriate. A stale or empty native tooltip is repaired on document/selection refresh, without per-frame geometry queries. Players never receive a caption marking an Undetected enemy's actual square.

## Supported manual spell saves

Native saving throws against **Laughing Fit, Stupefy and Blindness** use the same ongoing-effect resolver as the new NPC kit, regardless of whether the caster or defender is a PC. Roll normally from the spell/save workflow with a valid spell origin. The module marks only these saves to skip external duplicate outcome automation, awaits the native roll and asks the active GM to apply its source-tracked effects. It does not roll another save or apply manual spell damage. Other spells keep their existing automation.

The GM verifies the message author, target ownership, saving-throw type, spell UUID and active scene. Repeat requests for the same roll are idempotent. AI-managed saves use their own awaited execution path and are not handled twice. Rerolled saves currently require GM correction of the original effect; the handler does not silently replace an already resolved result.

## Sustain and duration

Select the caster and use the **Ongoing spells / Sustain** wand toolbar button. Choose the spell and Sustain, or end it. Player action counts remain manual: each Sustain costs one action. Only an owning player or the GM can request it, and the active GM resolves effects on other actors. Sustain is available during the caster's active combat turn and once per spell per turn.

Laughing Fit continues through the end of the caster's next turn. Each Sustain extends it through the following turn, to a maximum of ten minutes. Skipping Sustain ends its owned target effects. Its critical failure first denies actions/reactions and applies ordinary Prone; at the caster's next start it changes to Slowed 1 plus reaction denial. Ending the spell does not stand the target up.

Stupefy and Void Warp use caster-start expiry for their brief outcomes. Brief Blindness uses the target's next start instead. Minute-long outcomes use world time; permanent Blindness has no automatic expiry. Temporary Blindness immunity has its own one-minute marker and survives the brief Blinded outcome. The resolver checks the actual ended round at a round boundary, handles skipped turn-end events, and serializes lifecycle updates through the same barrier as reactions. Outside combat, unsupported continued upkeep expires as world time advances; the Sustain UI is currently a combat workflow.

All granted conditions belong to their spell effect. Ending or expiring that effect preserves unrelated sources. Prototype reset clears caster plans and removes their ongoing spells from other selected or affected tokens. Confused NPC behavior is supported from 0.37.0. Arbitrary lingering spells, Phantom Pain's linked Sickened/persistent damage, generic Dispel Magic/counteracting and automatic control of player turns remain outside this release.

Rules were checked against the supplied native spell items and [Laughing Fit on Archives of Nethys](https://2e.aonprd.com/Spells.aspx?ID=1583). Live encounter participants were not rolled for, damaged or moved during those inspections.
